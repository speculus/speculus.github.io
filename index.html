Missed opportunity

Good handicapping is not a guarantee of profit if you fail at the last hurdle. Here is my own failing.

In my audio clip for Race 4 on the Pune card of Thursday, 5 November 2026, I picked out Staria. Focus on what I said at 4:16 in the clip: I know exactly how she should look in the paddock, so if I choose to play, she will deliver.

But a medical emergency involving someone close to me kept me in Mumbai that day, and I could not travel to Pune. I had to depend on the live paddock camera at Pune, and it never showed the jockey mounting Staria. For me, that moment is essential to judge a horse's readiness. Without it, I could not make the final decision, and I missed Staria at 7/1.

After all, I am also human. — Prakash Gosavi

Rampage for General Rajendrasinhji Million

By Prakash Gosavi · First race at 2.00 pm

The Pune racecourse.

PUNE: For mid-week racing, the Royal Western India Turf Club (RWITC) Ltd has framed a seven-race card, and the General Rajendrasinhji Million is slated as the prime event. Of the seven in the fray, Rampage, Miami Vice, Noraiah and Victory Flair have all run good races this season, so this quartet is likely to throw up the eventual winner.

But I feel it would be the Shazaan Shah-trained Rampage, because he enjoys a rather decisive edge on the Dynamic Class Factor, based on his solitary run on this track behind Jaandaar who later went on to win the Villoo C Poonawalla Pune Derby (Gr 1).
