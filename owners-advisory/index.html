Special service

Exclusively for Horse-Owners

Private, data-driven insights into your horse

Private · Confidential · Only by appointment

Owning a racehorse is a passion and a significant investment. Prakash Gosavi's decades of scientific research into what makes a horse run faster, including a horse's stride, the K Factor (a stamina indicator) and the Dynamic Class Factor, can help you understand your horse better: its strengths, the conditions that suit it best, and its true potential.

What we can study for you

  • The race conditions your horse is best suited to: trip, weight, going and pace
  • Form patterns that point to an upcoming peak performance
  • Your horse's potential, before you buy or sell
  • A long-term view of how your horse is likely to progress
